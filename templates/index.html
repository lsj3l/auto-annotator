THE WORKFLOW

A considered reading process

How Auto Annotator transforms static readings into active margin conversations.

STEP 01

Extract & Analyze

Text layers are mapped line-by-line. Gemini reads the prose to identify core arguments, counter-evidence, and key distinctions worth questioning.

STEP 02

Make Room for Ink

The layout engine measures existing margins and expands blank space when needed without covering your source text. Each note anchors to its exact source line.

STEP 03

Render Genuine Handwriting

Pretrained neural pen strokes and translucent highlights are rendered into a standardized 8.5×11″ US Letter portrait document, ready for study or print.

Privacy & Data Handling

Your document: Files are processed in transient temporary memory during your session. Uploaded PDFs, page extracts, and finished documents are not retained on our servers.

Your API key: Keys are used strictly for in-flight requests to Google’s Gemini API and are never stored in your browser’s local storage or persistent server databases.

The engine: Handwriting synthesis uses the open-source pretrained model from Handwriter. Scanned and long readings up to 100 pages are fully supported with automated retry handling.
