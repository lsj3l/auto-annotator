A note on your notes.

The hosted app processes your document on its server. AI mode sends extracted reading text to Google’s Gemini API using the key you supply. The app does not intentionally retain hosted documents or keys, and it does not save your key in browser storage. Hosting and API providers have their own data and logging policies. Use readings you are allowed to process, and review AI-generated notes against the original.

The sample uses an original synthetic reading with preset annotations and makes no AI request. Scanned PDFs and longer documents require the separate local workflow. The handwriting engine is pretrained software from Handwriter, not a model trained by this project.
